# Physics: Classical Mechanics and Energy Principles

## Newton's Laws of Motion
Classical mechanics studies the motion of bodies under the influence of forces. Sir Isaac Newton formulated the three fundamental laws:
1. **First Law (Inertia)**: An object remains at rest or continues in uniform motion in a straight line unless acted upon by a net external force.
2. **Second Law (Force and Acceleration)**: The rate of change of momentum of a body is directly proportional to the applied net force and occurs in the direction of the force:
$$F = m a$$
3. **Third Law (Action and Reaction)**: For every action (force), there is an equal and opposite reaction.

## Conservation of Mechanical Energy
In an isolated system where only conservative forces (such as gravitational or spring forces) act, total mechanical energy $E$ is conserved:
$$E = K + U = \text{constant}$$
where:
- $K = \frac{1}{2} m v^2$ is the kinetic energy of the moving mass.
- $U = m g h$ is the gravitational potential energy relative to a reference plane.
Non-conservative forces (friction, air resistance) convert mechanical energy into thermal energy.
